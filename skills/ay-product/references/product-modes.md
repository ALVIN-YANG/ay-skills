# Product modes

Read only the mode that matches the current decision.

## New or broad idea

Research the user problem and current alternatives before committing to a solution. Compare plausible wedges, then recommend one user, job, switching trigger, MVP boundary, and cheapest risky-assumption test. A stop or validation-first recommendation is valid.

## Existing product

Diagnose observed behavior before proposing new scope. Use available analytics, feedback, support evidence, funnels, and current product behavior. Separate a usability or reliability problem from a positioning problem. Preserve approved behavior outside the demonstrated constraint.

## Fixed brief

Do not reopen settled strategy. Clarify only missing behavior, state transitions, permissions, failure recovery, and acceptance evidence. Record assumptions when the user explicitly asks the agent to choose.

## Validation only

Do not write invented implementation requirements. Define a bounded test with target participants or traffic, observable signal, denominator, time window, guardrails, and explicit build, revise, and stop rules.

## Sensitive product

Before recommending scope, cover data collected, access, consent, retention, deletion, recourse, abuse cases, safe defaults, and unresolved legal or policy dependencies. Do not turn a plausible safeguard into a claim of compliance.

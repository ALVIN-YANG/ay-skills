# Chinese social writing

Match the reader, account voice, purpose, platform, and requested deliverable. Platform conventions guide presentation; they do not require a persona, emojis, title batches, engagement bait, or a fixed template. A title-only revision stays title-only. A complete brief needs no outline approval.

Keep the title, opening, and content consistent: curiosity must earn a truthful payoff. Preserve source attribution and relevant limitations even in short copy. Do not turn supplied third-party observations into the author's experience, or manufacture purchases, emotions, before/after results, popularity, or testimonials. Missing experience can become an attributed explanation; ask for material only when it is essential to the requested form. Do not promise viral reach, conversion, algorithm preference, or detector evasion. Check current platform specifications only when the deliverable depends on them.

## WeChat articles · 公众号

Choose an angle that gives the intended reader a reason to continue. Open with a concrete scene, question, or supported judgment; develop it with connected reasoning and useful examples. Keep paragraphs comfortable on a phone. Match structure to the article's purpose: an opinion needs evidence, a story needs progression, a tutorial needs workable steps. End when the promise is fulfilled; add a relevant next action only when useful. Do not force technical modules, an abstract, repeated summaries, or subscription appeals.

## Xiaohongshu notes · 小红书

Coordinate the title, first-card text, and body around one specific reader need. Make each serve its place rather than repeat identical copy. Use compact paragraphs or scannable steps, concrete details, and clear suitability limits. Add relevant topics when requested or useful, without keyword stuffing. Match the author's voice; a note need not sound like an advertisement or use emojis. Cover copy is text, not a generated cover image.

## Video scripts · 视频文案、口播

Write for listening: natural spoken clauses, understandable terms, and a concrete opening whose promise the script delivers. Adapt the argument instead of reading an article verbatim. Budget spoken words together with pauses, demonstrations, and transitions; shorten content before accelerating delivery. Check the actual spoken text against the duration using a stated pace or available recording. Label estimated timing; timecodes alone do not prove fit.

A simple voiceover needs spoken copy, not a production kit. When a storyboard is requested, distinguish speech, visible action, and subtitle emphasis, with feasible shot durations and materials. Do not turn a Markdown table into a claimed subtitle file or completed video. Writing approval does not authorize rendering, account operations, or publishing.
